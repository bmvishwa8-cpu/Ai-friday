03 Enterprise Architecture & ServiceNow Integration

Seamless Integration with ServiceNow (SNOW)

Friday AI does not replace your system of record — it supercharges ServiceNow ITSM by injecting real-time AI intelligence directly into the ServiceNow Incident Management and Knowledge Management tables.

01
SNOW Incident Ingestion
Incoming ServiceNow incident created via Email / Service Portal triggers an automated Webhook / REST payload to Friday AI.
02
AI Triage & Diagnosis
Friday AI parses description, customer SLA, stack traces, and screenshots to diagnose technical root cause and specialized team.
03
SNOW Field Auto-Fill
Auto-populates ServiceNow assignment_group, assigned_to, category, urgency, and draft work notes.
04
Auto-KB Article Sync
Novel incident resolutions are packaged and pushed straight to the ServiceNow Knowledge Base (kb_knowledge table).

Key ServiceNow APIs & Entities Mapped:

  • /api/now/table/incident: Bi-directional sync of incident status, priority, and SLA deadlines.
  • sys_user_group: Automated routing to Tier 1, Database DBA, IAM, Billing, or Integrations groups.
  • sys_attachment: Computer-vision inference over error screenshot attachments.
  • kb_knowledge: Auto-generating standard operating playbooks directly into ServiceNow KB.

Zero Workflow Disruption:

Support agents can choose to work inside the modern, ultra-responsive Friday AI Triage Desk or remain entirely inside the native ServiceNow Agent Workspace. Every action, approval, and AI draft reflects bi-directionally in real-time.

05 Edge Cases, Limitations & Continuous Learning

What Happens If an Issue Is Brand-New & Not in the KB?

Traditional rule-based chatbots fail completely when an issue isn't in their database. Friday AI uses Large & Small Language Models (LLMs/SLMs) for semantic reasoning, autonomous KB generation, and self-learning.

The Limitation of Traditional Systems:

❌ Rigid keyword matching fails if customer describes symptoms using non-standard wording.
❌ When a new bug appears (zero existing KB articles), traditional systems stall or misroute the ticket to the wrong team, causing SLA violations.

How Friday AI Overcomes This:

1. Semantic Reasoning over Error Traces: Rather than looking for exact words, the LLM analyzes stack traces, API 401/500 codes, and UI screenshots to deduce the root cause.
2. Autonomous KB Article Generation: When a novel issue is diagnosed, Friday AI clicks into action and drafts a complete Standard Operating Playbook (SOP) with title, symptoms, root cause, and 4-step remediation.
3. Human-in-the-Loop Safeguard: If confidence is < 75%, it automatically flags as "Novel Incident - Human Review Needed" and routes to senior lead.

The Self-Healing Knowledge Loop

DAY 0
